Game guides and controls

The same characters appear in three different games. Each has a different objective. Choose a game below to learn what to watch before you play.

01

Pot Breaker RE

Guide Gimyo through an office maze and face Director Keuda.

Getting started

Choose a difficulty and enter the office. On a computer, use the arrow keys or WASD to move, Space to throw a plant pot, and Shift to dash three squares. Dash has a three-second cooldown. On a touch screen, use the direction buttons and the throw and dash controls. Hold a direction to keep moving.

Reading the screen

Gimyo's health and remaining pots appear on the left; Keuda's status appears on the right. Check your supply before throwing. The office partitions block routes, so a direct line toward the opponent is not always a path you can take.

A useful first approach

  1. Find a route with room to retreat before closing the distance.
  2. Keep moving until an attack lane opens, then throw a pot.
  3. Before entering a dead end, identify a direction you can dash back out.

The live health and attack indicators determine the outcome. Enemy behavior can vary with difficulty, so follow the current in-game display.

02

Ginkgo Tree Breaker

A 60-second reaction game about picking the right fruit.

What to tap

Tap yellow ginkgo fruit with a finger or click it with a mouse. Consecutive correct picks build a combo. A leaf breaks that streak, and a rotten fruit briefly locks input. Check the current appearance of each square instead of tapping every moving object.

Time and difficulty

A run lasts 60 seconds. Choose NORMAL, HARD, or VERY HARD. At the end, the game shows your score and best combo. You can choose whether to submit a ranking entry from the result screen.

A steadier scoring rhythm

  1. Identify the yellow fruit before tapping.
  2. Avoid leaves and rotten fruit to keep the combo going.
  3. If input is locked, wait for the next clear target rather than tapping repeatedly.

03

Floor Breaker

Four teams compete for the crown on an 11×11 board.

Each team starts with six pawns, three knights, and one queen. The first player is random. Move one piece per turn. You can face three AI teams or share one device with three other players. There is no online matchmaking.

How to win

A pawn normally claims the neutral crown in the center. The team holding it at the end of 30 complete rounds wins. If the crown is unclaimed then, play continues until a team claims it. Capturing the crown is only the beginning: the carrier can lose it under attack.

Taking a turn

Select a piece. A green dot marks a legal move; a red outline marks an attack. Tap the destination to move. The top bar shows the active team and remaining rounds. The side panel shows the crown owner and the number of pieces left. Each action has a ten-second limit.

What each piece does

PieceMoveAttack and crown
PawnOne square in any of eight directionsCan attack adjacent pawns, queens, and crown carriers. Normally claims the center crown.
KnightUp to two squares in any directionCaptures pawns and knights. Normally cannot claim the crown or attack a queen.
QueenUp to two squares in any directionDemotes a crown carrier to a knight or a knight to a pawn. Moves onto the target square while the demoted piece moves to a nearby empty square.

A crown carrier cannot attack pawns, but can capture knights and queens. When a queen demotes a carrier, the crown returns to the center or the nearest empty square if the center is occupied.

One-use character abilities

  • Gimyo: Doubles a pawn or knight move and jumps over enemies twice per game. A pawn still attacks only an adjacent square.
  • Keuda: Lets one pawn attack adjacent enemies in succession once per game.
  • Yi: Swaps one of your pieces with your pawn once per game.
  • Ho: Sacrifices the queen to let a knight claim the crown once per game.

Hidden blessings near the center grant an extra turn or add a pawn. A team holding the crown cannot collect a blessing, so the item remains on its square.

Questions players ask

Do I need to install anything?

All three games open in a browser. Pot Breaker RE and Ginkgo Tree Breaker download their game engine on first load, so the first start can take longer.

Can I play online with friends?

Floor Breaker offers AI play and four-player play on one device. Online matchmaking is not available.

Is my name put on a leaderboard automatically?

Ranking submission in Pot Breaker RE and Ginkgo Tree Breaker is a choice on the result screen. See the privacy and advertising notice for data details.
